# what's already here.

- **It runs with no accounts.** No database and no sign-in needed. Saved data waits until you want it.
- **Articles and stories.** Write them in a file or in the app. They come with their own layout, covers and link previews.
- **A worksheet for a problem.** Write down the idea, rank what matters and set a date.
- **Components with instructions.** Each one has an article saying what it does and what it tells you about your own build.
- **Setup steps, one at a time.** From an empty GitHub account to a live site with your name on it.
- **The parts most projects add last.** A sitemap, link previews, privacy and terms pages, and analytics that wait for consent.

# what you can do with it.

## make it say who you are

Your name, your colors, your words and your icons, redrawn by hand if you like. Most of it lives in one file.

:::badge[text=MAKE IT YOURS.,variant=primary,size=md,link=/article/setup-identity]:::

## write down what you're building

One problem at a time is easier to finish than a whole idea. Your first article or project page can start there.

:::badge[text=YOUR FIRST ARTICLE.,variant=primary,size=md,link=/article/setup-first-article]:::

:::badge[text=YOUR FIRST PROJECT PAGE.,variant=secondary,size=md,link=/article/setup-first-project-page]:::

## learn to work with a large language model, if you want

This is the part that stays with you. The code gives your LLM something real to read, so you can ask it what a file does, have it change one thing and check what it did. You keep the decisions.

:::badge[text=GET YOUR LLM.,variant=primary,size=md,link=/article/setup-get-your-claude]:::

## add the rest when you need it

Sign-in, a database, payments, email and search are each their own step. Skip what you don't need yet.
